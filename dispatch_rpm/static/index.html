Sources

    Every 12 hours the app reads DAT's weekly national reports and the U.S. diesel price. Rate = DAT linehaul + fuel surcharge, with the surcharge moving 1¢/mi per 6¢/gal of diesel. Numbers more than 20% away from the last accepted one are held back until another week's report confirms them.

    For rates a manager gives you. They're kept on this computer and replace the national average everywhere.
